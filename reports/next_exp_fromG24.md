# Next Experiment from EXP_G24: EXP_G25 Temporal Policy-Side Latent Memory

## Hypothesis

G24 shows that a visual predictive family is useful but that a memoryless aligned bottleneck is not necessary: aligned, unaligned, and direct visual controllers all tie at 9/10. A remaining role for coordinates is temporal rather than reconstructive. The hypothesis is that recurrent memory over predicted and realized visual-transition latents can identify proposal reliability, phase progress, and accumulated model error across decisions, improving closed-loop selection beyond both a memoryless visual scorer and a same-capacity recurrent model over raw state/action history.

This is new scientific content rather than another encoder coefficient. The intervention is moved into policy-side temporal conditioning; no latent is decoded into control. The exact native pi0.5 proposal selected by the temporal policy is the action executed in LIBERO.

## Sequential dataset construction

Build decision sequences from G24's 414 canonical checkpoints and 1,242 physical candidate branches. For each chronological decision, include the pre-decision dual-view embedding and physical state, all three exact native candidate prefixes, their predicted outcome features, the committed candidate, and the realized next visual/physical transition. Construct causal histories using only information available before the current selection. In particular, a committed transition may enter the history only after it was physically executed; uncommitted candidate outcomes may be targets during training but never observations at prospective test time.

Augment the ten existing trajectories with fresh matched controller rollouts when needed so every held-out attempt has real temporal histories for training and evaluation. Any added samples must be generated by actual checkpointed candidate intervention and saved with the same provenance as G24. No synthetic padding, teacher-forced future observation, or outcome leakage counts as evidence.

## Model families and mechanism ablations

Train leave-one-attempt-out temporal rankers with matched recurrent capacity:

1. a GRU policy over aligned action/visual-transition latent history, current visual state, and current candidate latents;
2. a GRU over raw native-action prefixes, physical state, and direct visual features, with parameter count matched to the latent GRU;
3. the G24 memoryless aligned visual scorer;
4. the G24 direct visual world model;
5. a latent-history shuffle ablation that preserves marginals but breaks chronological correspondence;
6. a history-reset ablation that clears recurrent state at every decision.

Predict candidate realized progress, success, physical displacement, and a calibrated model-residual penalty. Select architecture and loss from training folds only, then freeze all ten held-out models before prospective outcomes are read. Save sequence membership, causal masks, normalizers, checkpoints, per-step recurrent states, candidate scores, and held-out ranking/regret/calibration metrics.

## Prospective causal evaluation

From each of the ten canonical perturbed snapshots, run fresh full `lift -> place` rollouts with pi0.5 F1 and autonomous state-window F3. Compare:

1. aligned latent-history GRU;
2. capacity-matched raw-history GRU;
3. memoryless aligned visual scorer;
4. direct visual world model;
5. shuffled latent history;
6. reset-at-every-step latent history;
7. physical matched-branch shooting;
8. single raw pi0.5;
9. initial-observation open loop.

At every decision, save the exact pre-observation, history available to the ranker, all native proposals, recurrent states, scores, selected index, committed native action bytes, realized feedback, residual, F3 state, and terminal metric. All methods use matched seeds and initial simulator/controller checkpoints. Learned controllers execute no candidates for selection; physical shooting reports candidate-executed cost separately.

## Decision rule

Temporal latent conditioning contributes only if it strictly exceeds the raw-history GRU, memoryless aligned scorer, direct visual model, shuffled/reset ablations, and single raw pi0.5 in ordered success. It must also match or beat physical shooting with fewer candidate-executed steps, or strictly beat it. Ties do not support the claim. If raw-history recurrence wins or ties, retain the simpler representation and treat temporal memory rather than coordinates as the useful mechanism. If neither recurrent model improves on G24's 9/10, EXP_G26 must target the remaining failure through a new control or evaluation mechanism instead of tuning GRU width, seed, or threshold as a separate experiment.

# EXP_R2 interface audit

EXP_R2 keeps the EXP_R1 frozen representation, decoder, F1 and old F2. The 128-frame windows support H=2 and H=4 after the oracle midpoint; H=8 is not available and is explicitly not fabricated. Source goals are recovered from the immediately preceding same-session boundary record, while target sets and local radii use train sessions only.
